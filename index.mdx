---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Projekt Lemon is a Discord bot for moderation, tickets, verification and more, managed from a modern web dashboard."
---

Projekt Lemon brings tickets, verification, welcome messages, an embed maker, a level system, anti-raid protection, voice support and a web dashboard together in one bot.

<Note>
  Projekt Lemon is still under active development. Features may change as the bot evolves.
</Note>
