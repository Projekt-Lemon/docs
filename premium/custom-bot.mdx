---
title: "Custom bot"
description: "Run Projekt Lemon with your own name, avatar, status and activity."
---

With the custom bot you give Projekt Lemon your server's own identity. All modules work exactly the same, only the bot looks like yours.

## What you can customize

| Setting | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Bot name | Projekt Lemon |
| Avatar | Your server logo |
| Status | Online |
| Activity | Playing |
| Activity text | Lemon Modules |

## Switch the runtime

You can switch between your custom bot and the official Projekt Lemon bot at any time. **All module settings are kept** when you switch.

## Pricing and payment

The custom bot is a paid feature. Payments are processed through Stripe. Prices, terms and cancellation are described in the payment terms on [projektlemon.de](https://projektlemon.de).
