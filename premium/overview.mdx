Premium extends the modules that are already part of Projekt Lemon. It doesn't add separate systems. It removes the limits in the [web dashboard](/dashboard/overview).

## What Premium gives you

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No dashboard limits" icon="infinity">
    Without Premium, many settings are limited. Premium unlocks unlimited configuration for the existing systems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premium AI for tickets" icon="sparkles" href="/modules/tickets#ticket-ai">
    Select the premium AI model for [Ticket AI](/modules/tickets#ticket-ai).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Free vs. Premium

| Feature | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tickets](/modules/tickets) | Limited | Unlimited |
| Reaction roles | Limited | Unlimited |
| [Level rewards](/modules/leveling) | Limited | Unlimited |
| Other module settings in the dashboard | Limited | Unlimited |
| Ticket AI model | Standard | Premium AI available |

## Custom bot

Want the bot to run under your own name and avatar? See [Custom bot](/premium/custom-bot).

## Price

Premium costs **4,99 € per month** per server. The [ticket system](/modules/tickets) requires Premium.

## Get Premium

1. Open your server in the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview).
2. Select **Premium freischalten** and complete the checkout.

Premium is activated for the server as soon as the payment is completed.

### Premium role on the support server

On the Projekt Lemon support server, select **Premium Rolle beanspruchen** to receive the Premium role. The bot checks whether your Discord account has an active subscription.

## Payment

Payments are processed through Stripe. Prices, terms and cancellation are described in the payment terms on [projektlemon.de](https://projektlemon.de).
