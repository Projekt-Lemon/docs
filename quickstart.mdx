## Requirements

- A Discord server where you have the **Manage Server** permission.

## Set up Projekt Lemon

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite the bot">
    Open [projektlemon.de](https://projektlemon.de) or the bot's [Top.gg page](https://top.gg/bot/1392902571223421068) and select **Invite**. Choose your server and confirm the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Go to [projektlemon.de](https://projektlemon.de), sign in with your Discord account and select your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable modules">
    Turn on the modules you need, for example [welcome messages](/modules/welcome-messages), [tickets](/modules/tickets) or [verification](/modules/verification), and adjust their settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the role order">
    In Discord, open **Server Settings > Roles** and drag the Projekt Lemon role above every role the bot should assign or moderate. Discord only lets bots manage roles below their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Projekt Lemon uses slash commands. Type `/` in any channel to see the available commands.
</Tip>
