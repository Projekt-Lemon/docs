---
title: "Leveling"
description: "Reward active members with levels and rank cards."
---

The level system rewards activity on your server. Members level up as they participate and can show their progress with a rank card.

## Features

- Levels based on member activity
- **Rank cards** that display a member's level and progress

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Leveling** and enable the module.
3. Adjust the settings and save.
