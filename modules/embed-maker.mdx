The embed maker lets you build rich Discord embeds without writing code, for announcements, rules or info channels.

## Features

- Visual editor for titles, descriptions, colors, images and fields
- **Discord buttons** attached to your embeds

## Create an embed

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Embed maker**.
3. Design your embed and add buttons.
4. Choose a channel and send it.
