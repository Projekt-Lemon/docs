Projekt Lemon bundles several security tools that work together to keep your server safe.

## Anti-raid

Detects and stops raids, when many accounts join or spam at once.

## Auto-mod

Automatically moderates messages. Adaptive cooldowns slow down spammers without bothering regular members.

## Honeypot

A trap channel for spam bots. Accounts that post in it are banned automatically, and the dashboard shows statistics on caught accounts.

## Ghost ping log

Logs mentions that were deleted right after being sent, so members can't ping others and hide it. Choose a log channel, for example `#mod-log`.

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Security**.
3. Enable the tools you want and choose a log channel.

<Note>
  Combine security with [verification](/modules/verification) for the strongest protection.
</Note>
