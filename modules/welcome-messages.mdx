---
title: "Welcome messages"
description: "Greet new members with personal embeds, variables and optional components."
---

Welcome messages give new members a warm first impression and point them straight to your rules and most important channels.

## Features

- **Embeds** with a clean layout
- **Variables** that are replaced automatically, for example the member count
- **Optional components** such as buttons that link to rules or channels

## Example

```text
Welcome to the server!
You are member #{member}. Please read the rules!
```

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Welcome messages** and enable the module.
3. Choose the channel where the message should be posted.
4. Design your embed, add variables and components, then save.
