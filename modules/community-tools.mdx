All community tools are managed centrally in the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview).

## Giveaways

Host giveaways for your members directly in Discord.

## Forms

Collect structured information from members, for example applications or feedback.

## Team list

Show your server team in one place so members know who to contact.

## TempVoice

Let members create temporary voice channels that disappear when they are empty.

## Voice support

Offer support in voice channels. When a member joins the waiting channel:

1. The bot posts `🔔 <name> ist dem Support-Warteraum beigetreten.` in the staff notification channel.
2. The bot joins the waiting channel and plays a voice message: the **greeting voice** during support hours, the **offline voice** outside them.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Enabled | Turns voice support on or off |
| Waiting channel | Voice channel members join for support |
| Staff notification channel | Text channel for staff alerts |
| Greeting voice | MP3 played during support hours. Upload your own `.mp3` or pick an existing one. |
| Offline voice | MP3 played outside support hours |
| Support hours | A **from** and **to** time for each weekday. Days without times count as closed. |
| `/supporttimes` display | Show support hours only via `/supporttimes` (embed), in a channel, or by DM |
| `/supporttimes` command | Enables the slash command on the server |

<Note>
  Support hours use the bot server's system time.
</Note>
