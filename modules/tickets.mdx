The ticket system keeps support out of your public channels. Members open a ticket from a panel and your team handles it in a private channel.

## Features

  <Card title="Panels" icon="table-columns">

  <Card title="Categories" icon="folder">
    Sort tickets by topic.

  <Card title="Claim" icon="hand">

  <Card title="Transcripts" icon="file-lines">

  <Card title="Close request" icon="circle-xmark">

## Ticket AI

Ticket AI is optional. It answers questions using your server's knowledge base and can look things up on the web when needed. Your team can take over at any time.

With [Premium](/premium/overview), you can select the premium AI model for Ticket AI.

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Tickets** and enable the module.
3. Create one or more categories.
4. Create a panel and post it in a channel.
5. Optionally enable Ticket AI and fill your knowledge base.
