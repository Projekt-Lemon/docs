---
title: "Verification"
description: "Let new members verify with a button and receive a role automatically."
---

Verification posts an embed with a **Verify** button. Members who click it receive the verification role.

## Settings

| Field | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Channel | Channel the verification embed is posted to |
| Role | Role members receive after verifying |
| Title | `Verifizierung` |
| Description | `Klicke auf den Button unten, um dich zu verifizieren.` |
| Color | `#3b82f6` |
| Button text | `Verifizieren` |

When you save, the bot sends the embed with a green button to the selected channel. The button keeps working after a bot restart.

## What members see

All replies are only visible to the member who clicked:

- **Success:** the member receives the role.
- **Already verified:** the member already has the role.
- **No role configured** or **role no longer exists:** an error is shown.
- **Missing permissions:** the bot cannot assign the role.

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Verification**.
3. Select the channel and the role, then adjust the text and save.
4. Make sure the Projekt Lemon role is above the verification role in **Server Settings > Roles**.
