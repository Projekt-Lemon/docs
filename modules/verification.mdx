---

Verification makes new members prove they are human before they get access to your server.

## Image captcha

Members solve an image captcha to verify. You choose the difficulty, from **easy** up to **extreme**, which is highly resistant to automated text recognition (OCR).

<Tip>
  Start with a lower level and raise it if automated accounts still get through.
</Tip>

## Set up

1. Open the [dashboard](/dashboard/overview) and select your server.
2. Open **Verification** and enable the module.
3. Choose the captcha difficulty.
4. Select the role members receive after verifying.
5. Make sure the Projekt Lemon role is above that role in **Server Settings > Roles**.
